Mutton Fry Thali

An Ode to the Maratha Legacy

The Culinary Heritage

Born in the royal kitchens of the Maratha empire, the Mutton Fry Thali is more than just food—it is a ritual. The legendary combination of the fiery Tambda and the soothing Pandhara Rassa was designed to balance the body's humors, ensuring that every bite is a journey from intense heat to cooling bliss.
