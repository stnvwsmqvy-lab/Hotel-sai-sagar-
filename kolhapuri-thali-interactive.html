Mutton Fry Thali

An Ode to the Maratha Legacy

The Culinary Heritage

Born in the royal kitchens of the Maratha empire, the Mutton Fry Thali is more than just food—it is a ritual. The legendary combination of the fiery Tambda and the soothing Pandhara Rassa was designed to balance the body's humors, ensuring that every bite is a journey from intense heat to cooling bliss.

The Complete Plate

Tambda Rassa

The fiery red starter curry packed with heat and Byadgi chilies to wake the soul.

Pandhara Rassa

White, coconut-based gravy to balance the spice with its soothing aroma.

Mutton Fry

Dry, spicy shallow-fried mutton. The hero of the plate with a crisp crust.

Dahi Kanda

Refreshing onion and yogurt salad essential for digestion after a heavy meal.

Anda Curry

Egg halves simmered in a rich masala gravy, adding savory depth.

Bhakri / Puri

Traditional Maharashtrian bread - crispy puri or jowar bhakri to scoop up the flavors.
