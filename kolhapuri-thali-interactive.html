The Mutton Fry Thali

Rooted in the royal kitchens of the Maratha Empire and the vibrant streets of Kolhapur, the Mutton Fry Thali is not just a meal—it's a legacy. Historically, the combination of Tambda and Pandhara Rassa was developed to balance the fiery palate of the warriors with the soothing properties of mild gravies. It represents the agrarian richness of Maharashtra, where spices are not just flavor, but medicine.
